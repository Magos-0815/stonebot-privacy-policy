Privacy Policy

This policy explains how RossoBot processes information when a deployment uses WhatsApp or another user-selected integration.

Effective: 9 August 2026 Last updated: 9 August 2026
Independent project. RossoBot is independently operated software, not a product of or endorsed by Meta or WhatsApp. The operator of each RossoBot deployment controls that deployment and is responsible for its use.

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to the RossoBot application and its WhatsApp integration. For the Meta application linked to this page, the project operator is identified by the GitHub account Magos-0815, acting as an independent project operator. If another person or organisation deploys RossoBot, that deployment operator is the controller of information in its environment and may provide additional terms.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features enabled by the deployment operator, RossoBot may process:

  • WhatsApp identifiers: phone-number identifiers, WhatsApp account or business identifiers, profile name, and conversation participants.
  • Message information: messages sent to or from the connected WhatsApp account and, when explicitly supported, media or document references.
  • Message metadata: message and thread identifiers, timestamps, direction, delivery status, and reply context.
  • Workflow records: drafts, action requests, approval decisions, execution receipts, and audit records needed to show what was requested and done.
  • Technical and security data: connection events, finite error codes, service health information, and security events.
  • Connected-service data: information a user asks RossoBot to read from or write to another integration. This is processed only when that integration is configured and the relevant action is requested.

Authentication credentials and access tokens may be required to connect services. They are intended to be kept in the deployment's protected credential store and are not published in this policy repository.

3. Where information comes from

Information may come directly from a user, from the deployment operator, from Meta's WhatsApp APIs and webhooks, or from another service the user has chosen to connect.

4. How information is used

RossoBot processes information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • receive, display, organise, and respond to messages;
  • prepare drafts and perform actions requested by an authorised user;
  • require approval before external write actions where the deployment enables that control;
  • deliver messages and report delivery or execution results;
  • prevent duplicate actions, abuse, fraud, and unauthorised access;
  • maintain reliability, troubleshooting information, and an auditable action history; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

RossoBot does not sell personal information and does not use WhatsApp message content for third-party advertising.

5. Legal grounds

The applicable legal ground depends on the deployment and jurisdiction. It may include performing a service requested by the user, consent, the operator's legitimate interest in providing and securing the service, or compliance with law. Deployment operators must obtain any consent or opt-in required for their use of WhatsApp and RossoBot.

6. When information is shared

Information may be disclosed only as needed to:

  • Meta and WhatsApp, which transport WhatsApp messages and process data under their own terms and privacy policies;
  • deployment infrastructure providers, such as hosting, storage, or security providers selected by the deployment operator;
  • user-selected integrations, when an authorised user asks RossoBot to perform an action involving that service; or
  • authorities or other parties, when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and service integrity.

RossoBot does not rent or sell personal information to data brokers.

7. Storage and retention

RossoBot is designed for local-first operation. Information is normally stored in the environment controlled by the deployment operator, although the operator may choose hosted infrastructure or other processors. Retention depends on the deployment's settings, the user's request, security needs, and applicable law. Credentials are retained until disconnected or revoked. Message and workflow records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for the requested service, troubleshooting, security, and audit integrity, then deleted or de-identified where feasible.

8. Security

RossoBot uses safeguards appropriate to its deployment, which may include encrypted HTTPS transport, access controls, protected credential storage, redacted logs, approval gates, idempotency controls, and execution receipts. No system can guarantee absolute security. Deployment operators are responsible for securing their own environment and credentials.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability, and may object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. A user can also stop sending messages, block the WhatsApp account, or ask the deployment operator to disconnect the integration. See the Data Deletion Instructions for the request process.

10. Children

RossoBot is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required by local law. Deployment operators must not knowingly use RossoBot to collect children's personal information without valid authorisation.

11. International processing

Meta, hosting providers, and connected services may process information in countries other than the user's own. Deployment operators must use appropriate safeguards where required.

12. This policy website

This static website does not use advertising cookies or project-operated analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process ordinary web-server information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

13. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date. When appropriate, deployment operators should also notify affected users through the service.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open a privacy request ticket. The ticket is public: do not include a phone number, message content, credentials, or other personal information. Ask the project operator to establish a private channel. For a third-party RossoBot deployment, contact that deployment's operator first because the public policy site cannot access its data.
