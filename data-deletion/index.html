Data Deletion Instructions

Use this process to ask for deletion of information processed by Stonebot.

Last updated: 9 August 2026
Protect your privacy. Never put your phone number, messages, credentials, access tokens, or identity documents in a public GitHub ticket.

Step 1 — Identify the deployment operator

If you interacted with a Stonebot deployment run by another person or organisation, contact that operator first. This public static website does not receive WhatsApp messages and cannot access data held in a separate deployment.

Step 2 — Start a request without personal data

For the Stonebot project connected to this site, open a privacy request ticket. Choose “Data deletion” and request a private contact channel. Do not identify the WhatsApp account or describe the messages in the public ticket.

Step 3 — Verify the request privately

The deployment operator will provide a private channel and may request limited information needed to locate the relevant record and verify that the requester is entitled to delete it. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, or access tokens.

Step 4 — Deletion and confirmation

A verified request may cover, where applicable:

  • WhatsApp identifiers and profile information held by the Stonebot deployment;
  • inbound and outbound message content or locally stored media;
  • conversation, draft, action-request, and execution-receipt records;
  • connected-account credentials or tokens controlled by the deployment; and
  • technical records that can reasonably be associated with the requester.

Verified requests will be handled without undue delay and generally within 30 days. Limited records may be retained when required by law or when reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or audit integrity. Where possible, retained records will be minimised or de-identified. The operator will confirm the outcome or explain any lawful limitation.

Data held by Meta or another service

This process covers data controlled by the relevant Stonebot deployment. Information independently held by WhatsApp, Meta, GitHub, a hosting provider, or another connected service is governed by that provider's own deletion tools and privacy policy.

中文说明

如何申请删除数据

  1. 先确认与你互动的 Stonebot 部署由谁运营；第三方部署的数据应联系该部署运营者。
  2. 在本项目的公开工单中只选择“数据删除”并请求私密联系方式。不要公开填写电话号码、 消息、凭据或身份证明。
  3. 运营者会通过私密渠道，以最少必要信息核验身份并定位数据。
  4. 核验后的申请通常在 30 天内处理，并告知删除结果或依法需要保留的有限内容。
